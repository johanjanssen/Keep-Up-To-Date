What Would Have Limited the Damage?

  • 🔒No root access
  • 🐳A minimal, up-to-date base image
  • 📦No unneeded OS tools: curl, wget, gcc, pkexec
  • 📚Up-to-date application dependencies

🚨 Most of Your Application Isn't Your Code

  • 🧩Hundreds of direct and transitive dependencies, plus the OS, runtime and base image
  • 🎲Every one of them can ship the next CVE
  • ⏳Code you didn't write still ages, even when you don't touch it

The Compounding Cost of Delay

  • 🔐Security: when a CVE forces your hand, you jump five versions under pressure instead of one
  • ⚡Performance: every release you skip is free speed you don't get
  • 🧗Migration effort: ten small hops are cheaper than one big leap

🇦🇺 Australia already issued its first multimillion-dollar fine

🎯 Your target: CVE published → fix in production in < 72 hours

Australia: Australian Clinical Labs, 2025, first-ever penalty under the Privacy Act: $5.8M. The 2022 breach itself (223,000 people) wasn't the trigger; a ~3-week delay notifying the regulator was. Medibank, ongoing: 9.7M people's health data exposed (2022). OAIC is suing for up to $2.22M per contravention; still before the Federal Court.

How's Your Situation?
