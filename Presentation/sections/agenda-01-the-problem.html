What Would Have Limited the Damage?

  • 🔒No root access
  • 🐳A minimal, up-to-date base image
  • 📦No unneeded OS tools: curl, wget, gcc, pkexec
  • 📚Up-to-date application dependencies

🚨 Most of Your Application Isn't Your Code

  • 🧩Direct and transitive dependencies, plus the OS, runtime and base image
  • 🎲Every one of them can ship the next security vulnerability
  • ⏳Code you didn't write still ages, even when you don't touch it

The Compounding Cost of Delay

  • 🔐Security: when a vulnerability forces your hand, you jump five versions under pressure instead of one
  • ⚡Performance: every release you skip is free speed you don't get
  • 🧗Migration effort: ten small hops are cheaper than one big leap

Newer Runtime, Free Speed

  • ⚙️Every runtime release brings compiler, GC and library improvements
  • 🐍Python 3.11: on average 25% faster than 3.10
  • 🟣.NET, Node.js, Go: faster with every release
  • 💤You only get it if you upgrade

⚖️ It's Not Just Best Practice Anymore

⚖️ EU Cyber Resilience Act

For software you sell in the EU, even if you're an Australian company

  • 📣Since Sep 2026: report actively exploited vulnerabilities within 24 hours
  • 🛡️From Dec 2027: ship without known exploitable vulnerabilities and provide security updates for the support period (usually at least 5 years)
  • 💶Breaking these rules: fines up to €15M or 2.5% of global turnover, whichever is higher, and your product can be pulled from the EU market

🇦🇺 Closer to Home

2025: Australian Clinical Labs fined A$5.8M, mostly for inadequate security
