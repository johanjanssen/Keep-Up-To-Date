Key Takeaways

🏁 Key Takeaways

  • 🗑️Remove what you don't need first: the cheapest dependency, or line of code, to maintain is the one that's gone
  • 🔄Update incrementally and frequently: big jumps are big risks
  • 📌Pin versions, not floating tags: reproducibility ≠ security
  • 🔍Scan both source and artifact: they find different vulnerabilities
  • 🤖Automate version bumps and code migrations: policy belongs in config, not in people's heads
  • 🧪Tests make automation safe: a bot or migration tool without tests is automated risk
  • 🛡️Smaller images = smaller attack surface: distroless / scratch = 0 OS CVEs
  • ⏱️MTTP is your metric: CVE published → production in < 72 hours
