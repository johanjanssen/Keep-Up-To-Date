Update or Remove?

Two Options

🗑️ Remove

The cheapest dependency to maintain is the one that's gone

🔄 Update

Keep what's left current, in small regular steps

Benefits of Removing or Updating

  • 🔒Security
  • 🔧Maintainability
  • ⬆️Upgradability
  • 😄Lots more words ending in -ity
  • 🔋Reduced resource consumption
  • ⚡Performance
  • ⚖️Adherence to laws and regulations
  • 💰Saving money
