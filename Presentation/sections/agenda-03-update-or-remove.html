Update or Remove?

Update or Remove?

  • 🗑️The cheapest dependency to maintain is the one that's gone
  • 🔄Update the required software regularly

Benefits of Removing or Updating

  • 🔒Security
  • 🔧Maintainability
  • ⬆️Upgradability
  • 😄Lots more words ending in -ity
  • 🔋Reduced resource consumption
  • ⚡Performance
  • ⚖️Adherence to laws and regulations
  • 💰Saving money

Before Removing: Prove It's Unused

Don't guess: measure. Track what your code actually calls in production before deleting it.
